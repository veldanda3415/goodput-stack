## saturation

- Throughput basis: `steady_output_tok_s` (only the window where every client is busy)
- Scaling: 1→49 tok/s (100% linear); 2→60 tok/s (61% linear, +22% vs 1); 4→73 tok/s (37% linear, +21% vs 2); 6→78 tok/s (26% linear, +6% vs 4); 8→78 tok/s (20% linear, +1% vs 6); 12→78 tok/s (13% linear, +0% vs 8); 16→92 tok/s (12% linear, +17% vs 12); 24→92 tok/s (8% linear, +0% vs 16)
- **Max throughput**: 92 tok/s at 24 clients (TPOT p50 163.0 ms, TTFT p95 19,754.8 ms)
- Repeat spread of throughput: up to 7.1% (stable)
- 90%-of-max point: 16 clients, 92 tok/s, TPOT p50 157.5 ms vs 15.6 ms at 1
- **Knee (max power = throughput / TPOT)**: **1 clients** - 49 tok/s at TPOT p50 15.6 ms; beyond it each extra client costs more latency than it adds throughput
- Slot queueing starts at 24 clients (max queued 8)
- **SLO optimum** (TTFT ≤ 2000 ms, TPOT ≤ 50 ms): max goodput 57 tok/s at 2 clients (100% of requests in SLO); highest load with ≥99% in SLO: 2 clients
